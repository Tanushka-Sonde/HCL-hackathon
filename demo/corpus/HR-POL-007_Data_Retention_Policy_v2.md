> SYNTHETIC DEMO DOCUMENT. Orion Synthetic Industries (OSI) is fictional.

## 1. Purpose

This policy defines how long OSI keeps business records and how records are disposed of once they are no longer needed. It applies to all employees, contractors and systems that create or store business records.

## 2. Retention Periods

The standard document retention period for business records is 7 years from the end of the financial year in which the record was created.

Financial records, including invoices and purchase orders, must be retained for 8 years.

Employee personnel files are retained for 6 years after the employee leaves OSI.

Email messages that are not business records are deleted automatically after 2 years.

## 3. Legal Hold

When the Legal team issues a Legal Hold (LH) notice, disposal is suspended for every affected record until Legal formally releases the hold, regardless of the normal retention period.

## 4. Disposal

Records that have passed their retention period must be disposed of within 90 days using approved secure disposal, either cross-cut shredding or certified data wiping. Every disposal is logged in the Records Disposal Register.

## 5. Changes from Version 1.0

Version 2.0 extends the standard retention period for business records and adds the 90-day disposal window. Version 1.0 is superseded and must not be used for new decisions.
