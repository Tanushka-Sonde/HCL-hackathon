> SYNTHETIC DEMO DOCUMENT. Orion Synthetic Industries (OSI) is fictional.

## 1. Booking Channel

All flights and hotels are booked through the corporate travel portal at least 14 days before departure where possible.

## 2. Cabin Class by Flight Duration

| Flight duration | Allowed cabin class |
| --- | --- |
| Under 6 hours | Economy |
| 6 to 10 hours | Premium economy |
| Over 10 hours | Business class with director approval |

## 3. Changes

Ticket changes caused by business needs are paid by the cost centre. Personal changes are paid by the traveller.
