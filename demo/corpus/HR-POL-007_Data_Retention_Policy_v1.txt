SYNTHETIC DEMO DOCUMENT. Orion Synthetic Industries (OSI) is fictional.

1 Purpose
This policy describes how long OSI keeps business records.

2 Retention Periods
The standard document retention period for business records is 5 years from the date the record was created.
Financial records, including invoices and purchase orders, are retained for 7 years.
Employee personnel files are retained for 5 years after the employee leaves OSI.

3 Disposal
Records past their retention period should be shredded or deleted by the record owner. No fixed disposal window applies.
