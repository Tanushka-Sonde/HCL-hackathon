# Remote Work Policy

Doc-ID: HR-POL-010
Title: Remote Work Policy
Category: HR
Version: 1.1
Status: effective
Effective: 2025-06-01

> SYNTHETIC DEMO DOCUMENT. Orion Synthetic Industries (OSI) is fictional.

## 1. Eligibility

Employees in corporate functions (HR, Finance, Legal, Procurement) may work remotely up to 2 days per week with their manager's approval. Employees in their probation period work on site.

## 2. Equipment

OSI provides a laptop and a headset. A one-time home office allowance of USD 300 is paid after the manager approves the remote work arrangement.

## 3. Working Hours

Remote employees must be reachable during core hours, 10:00 to 16:00 local time.
