Effective: 2025-02-01

SYNTHETIC DEMO DOCUMENT. Orion Synthetic Industries (OSI) is fictional.

1. CARD LIMITS
Standard corporate cards have a monthly limit of USD 5,000. Senior managers may request a monthly limit of USD 15,000.

2. RECEIPTS
Card transactions must be reconciled with receipts in the expense system within 15 days of the statement date. Unreconciled transactions after 45 days suspend the card.

3. PROHIBITED USE
Corporate cards must not be used for personal purchases, cash withdrawals or gift cards.
