# Engineering Remote Work Guidelines

Doc-ID: ENG-POL-014
Title: Engineering Remote Work Guidelines
Category: Engineering
Version: 2.0
Status: effective
Effective: 2025-08-15

> SYNTHETIC DEMO DOCUMENT. Orion Synthetic Industries (OSI) is fictional.

## 1. Remote Days for Engineers

Engineering staff may work remotely up to 3 days per week. Teams must agree one shared on-site day for planning.

## 2. On-call Engineers

Engineers on the on-call rota must be within 60 minutes of a reliable internet connection and must acknowledge SEV-1 pages within 15 minutes.

## 3. Equipment

Engineers may claim a second monitor once every 3 years, up to USD 250.
