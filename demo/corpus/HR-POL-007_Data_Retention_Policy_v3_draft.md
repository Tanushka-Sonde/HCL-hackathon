Category: HR

Status: draft
Effective: 2027-01-01

> SYNTHETIC DEMO DOCUMENT. Orion Synthetic Industries (OSI) is fictional.

## 1. Purpose of this draft

This draft proposes changes to the effective version 2.0 for review by the Legal team. It is not in force until approved.

## 2. Proposed Retention Periods

The draft proposes that standard business records be retained for 10 years instead of 7 years, to align with a new regional audit rule.
The draft proposes that recruitment records of unsuccessful candidates be deleted after 12 months.

## 3. Review

Comments on the draft are due by 15 December 2026 and go to the Records Management Office.
