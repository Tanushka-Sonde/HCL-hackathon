SYNTHETIC DEMO DOCUMENT. Orion Synthetic Industries (OSI) is fictional.

1. TRAINING BUDGET
Each employee has a learning budget of USD 1,500 per calendar year. Unused budget does not carry over to the next year.

2. CERTIFICATIONS
Professional certification exam fees are reimbursed once the exam is passed. Employees who leave within 12 months of a reimbursed certification repay half of the fee.

3. LEARNING TIME
Employees may spend up to 4 hours per month of working time on approved learning.
