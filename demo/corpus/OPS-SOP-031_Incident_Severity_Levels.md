# Incident Severity Levels

Doc-ID: OPS-SOP-031
Title: Incident Severity Levels
Category: Operations
Version: 1.4
Status: effective
Effective: 2025-05-10

> SYNTHETIC DEMO DOCUMENT. Orion Synthetic Industries (OSI) is fictional.

## 1. Severity Definitions

| Severity | Meaning | First response | Update cadence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| SEV-1 | Customer-facing outage of a production system | 15 minutes | every 30 minutes |
| SEV-2 | Major feature degraded, workaround exists | 1 hour | every 2 hours |
| SEV-3 | Minor issue with limited impact | 1 business day | daily |
| SEV-4 | Cosmetic issue or question | 3 business days | on resolution |

## 2. Escalation

A SEV-1 incident is escalated to the duty director after 60 minutes without mitigation. A post-incident review is required for every SEV-1 and SEV-2 incident within 5 business days.
