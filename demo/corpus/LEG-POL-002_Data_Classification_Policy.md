Title: Data Classification Policy
Category: Legal
Version: 1.0
Status: effective
Effective: 2024-11-01

> SYNTHETIC DEMO DOCUMENT. Orion Synthetic Industries (OSI) is fictional.

## 1. Classification Levels

OSI uses four levels: Public, Internal, Confidential and Restricted. Personally identifiable information (PII) is always at least Confidential.

## 2. Controlled Unclassified Information (CUI)

Customer contract data marked CUI must be stored only in approved systems in the EU region and must never be sent by personal email.

## 3. Labelling

Documents classified Confidential or Restricted must carry the classification label in the header of every page.
