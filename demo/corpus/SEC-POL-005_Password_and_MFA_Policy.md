# Password and MFA Policy

Doc-ID: SEC-POL-005
Title: Password and MFA Policy
Category: Security
Version: 1.2
Status: effective
Effective: 2025-04-01

> SYNTHETIC DEMO DOCUMENT. Orion Synthetic Industries (OSI) is fictional.

## 1. Passwords

Passwords must be at least 14 characters long. Passwords are not rotated on a schedule; they are changed only after a suspected compromise.

## 2. Multi-factor Authentication (MFA)

New employees must enroll in MFA within 5 business days of their start date. Hardware security keys are required for administrators.

## 3. Single Sign-On (SSO) and PAM

All business applications must use single sign-on (SSO). Privileged access management (PAM) sessions for administrators are recorded and kept for 1 year.
